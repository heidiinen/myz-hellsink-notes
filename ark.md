**The Ark**\
The Mutants have always lived inside the ruins known as Hydrabelle's Club. The name is incomplete because part of the original stone sign fell down long ago. Nobody knows what the missing part said.

**An Old Temple**\
The Mutants believe the site was once a vast temple built by the powerful people of the Old World. They worshipped Hydrabelle, the First Mutant, who they believed would save them from the wrath of the Mutants.

**Sacrificial Grounds**\
The land outside the Ark is covered in circular holes, believed to be ancient sacrificial altars. People of the Old World supposedly offered great, white pearls to Hydrabelle there. The Mutants avoid the holes, fearing whatever ancient powers may remain.

**The Club House**\
The huge, decaying clubhouse forms the heart of the temple. Its towering walls, enormous windows, balconies, and traces of gold suggest it was built to inspire awe.

**Statue of Hydrabelle**\
A gigantic golden statue of Hydrabelle stands beside the clubhouse, weapon raised as if eternally protecting the Ark. Her eyes seem to follow those who move around her. Water flows from her mouth into a basin, providing the mutants with their vital drinking water.

**Sacred Weapons**\
The Mutants' weapons are made in Hydrabelle's image. They are long clubs with heavy ends and round stones like the pearls of the ancients. Their origing remain unknown, but they are old and sacred and feel right in the hands when swung.

**The Grounds**\
A crude scrap-metal barrier surrounds the Ark. Beyond it are overgrown altars and sunken paths. At nights the Mutants sometimes hear unexplained clicking and rolling sounds, like pearls searching for the sacrificial holes. The Ark grounds are surrounded by forest and overgrown terrain in every direction.
