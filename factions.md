Three major factions compete for influence over the Ark. They constantly clash in the Council and around the Ark.

**The Collective**\
Advocates for fairness and communal ownership. The Dawn Vault belongs to everyone.

**Steelgrip**\
Believes strength and power should determine who leads. The Dawn Vault must stay sealed until someone strong enough can claim it.

**The Brokers**\
Values freedom and treats everything as something that can be traded or bought. Even forbidden knowledge, such as what's inside The Dawn Vault, has a price.

Before the rise of the factions, the Elder was the foundation holding the Ark together. He never needed force or formal control - his presence alone commanded respect. Unlike the Mutants, the Elder was physically human. Still, the Elder taught that the Old World was dead and should remain forgotten. He blamed its greed and uncontrolled knowledge for its destruction, leaving behing ruins, poison and wasteland. The message: do not look back.
